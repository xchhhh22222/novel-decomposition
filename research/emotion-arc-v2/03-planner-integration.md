# Optional Planner integration

V2 is an opt-in research design mode. The existing sequence remains valid:

`E0 → E1 → M0 → D1 → D2 → D3 → D4`

When explicitly selected, insert:

`E0 → E1 → E2 macro-arc design → E3 weaving and handoff → M0 → D1 → D2 → D3 → existing Story Spine / climax backplanning → D4`

The mode produces four linked planning views: series promise and macro arcs, active line states, weave/handoff contracts, and DNA material slots. It does not replace the nine-stage plotline lifecycle or duplicate a story summary across four files.

Research source candidates remain `ADAPTABLE` and retain their repository, commit, path, component ID, and limitations. Gaps remain `MATERIAL_GAP` or `ORIGINAL_DESIGN`. No research candidate becomes a production component through this adapter.

The detailed guide is `skills/novel-creation-planner/references/emotion-arc-weaving-v2.md`; the hypothetical fixture is intentionally outside formal plan-schema validation.
